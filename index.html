Jersey Tides Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 May 2026

Overview

Jersey Tides does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Tide Data

The app contacts a secure backend service to fetch tide prediction data for Jersey. These requests are used only to provide tide information in the app, widgets, and Live Activities.

Personal Data

The app does not require an account, does not collect your name or email address, does not track your location, and does not use third-party advertising or analytics.

Feedback

If you send feedback by email, your email address and message will only be used to respond to your feedback.

Important Notice

Tide prediction data is provided for general guidance only and should not be used as the sole source for navigation or safety-critical decisions. Always check local conditions and official marine information before heading out.
